    How It Works

    We keep it simple
    so you can stay focused.

    Influencer marketing shouldn't feel like a second job. We take every step off your plate, from finding the right match to negotiating the deal. You just say yes or no.

    01

    You join the network

    Whether you're a brand or a creator, getting into our network costs nothing. We take you on, learn what you're about, and keep you ready for the right moment.

    02

    We find your match

    We do the legwork. Researching, vetting, reaching out. When we find a pairing that makes real sense, aligned audience, aligned values, we bring it to you.

    03

    You pick who you work with

    We present the opportunity. You decide if it's right. No pressure, no obligation. If it doesn't feel right, we keep looking. You're always in control.

    04

    Deal closes, we take 15%

    Only when a deal is agreed and signed do we take our cut. No retainers, no setup fees, no monthly charges. If there's no deal, there's no cost.
