    We didn't set out to build another marketing agency. We set out to give New Zealand's gym creators the industry recognition they've always deserved.

    Where It Started

    A gap in the market.
    A decision to fill it.

    New Zealand has some of the most dedicated, authentic fitness creators in the world. Yet for too long, they've been overlooked by the brands that should have been backing them.

    Nixon Winger and Luke Singh saw this in 2026. Two young New Zealanders who both believed the NZ gym space deserved better. So they built Roots.

    The Journey

    How we got here.

    2026

    The idea takes root

    Nixon Winger and Luke Singh spotted the same problem independently. NZ's gym creators were producing world-class content and building loyal audiences, yet major brands continued to look straight past them. A conversation became a decision: do something about it. Roots was founded.

    Now

    Building from the ground up

    We're at the beginning, and we're proud of it. Nixon is building the systems and digital infrastructure that will power every partnership. Luke is out meeting creators and brands, laying the groundwork for relationships built on trust.

    Next

    First partnerships on the horizon

    We're actively seeking our first brand-creator partnerships within the NZ fitness space. If you're a gym creator with an engaged audience, or a brand that wants to reach one authentically, this is exactly the moment to get involved.
