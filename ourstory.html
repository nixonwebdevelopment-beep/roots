    Our Story

    Built for
    New Zealand's
    finest.

    We didn't set out to build another marketing agency. We set out to give New Zealand's gym creators the industry recognition they've always deserved.

    Where It Started

    A gap in the market.
    A decision to fill it.

    New Zealand has some of the most dedicated, authentic fitness creators in the world. Yet for too long, they've been overlooked by the brands that should have been backing them. The deals went offshore. The budgets went elsewhere. The talent went unrecognised.

    Nixon Winger and Luke Singh saw this in 2026. Two young New Zealanders, one from New Plymouth, one from Waiheke Island, who both believed the NZ gym space deserved better. Not more noise. Just the right connections, done properly.

    So they built Roots. A brand new, NZ-based agency dedicated entirely to bridging the gap between local fitness creators and the brands ready to invest in them. We're just getting started, and we think that's the best time to get involved.

    The Journey

    How we got here.

    2026

    The idea takes root

    Nixon Winger and Luke Singh spotted the same problem independently, New Zealand's gym creators were producing world-class content and building loyal audiences, yet major brands continued to look straight past them. A conversation between two passionate New Zealanders became a decision: do something about it. Roots was founded.

    Now

    Building from the ground up

    We're at the beginning, and we're proud of it. Nixon is building the systems, tools, and digital infrastructure that will power every partnership we facilitate. Luke is out meeting creators and brands, laying the groundwork for relationships built on trust. The foundation is being set carefully, because everything we build on top of it has to last.

    Next

    First partnerships on the horizon

    We're actively seeking our first brand-creator partnerships within the NZ fitness space. If you're a gym creator with an engaged audience, or a brand that wants to reach one authentically, this is exactly the moment to get involved. The ground floor won't be available forever.
